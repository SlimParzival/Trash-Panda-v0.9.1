RACCOON RAMPAGE — THE LEGEND OF TRASH PANDA
v0.9.1 — Combat & Art Pass

Key upgrades from v0.9:
- Trash Shotgun primary attack with satisfying spread/pellet feedback.
- Dumpster Flamethrower evolution for close-range fire.
- More coins from normal enemies and larger boss rewards.
- Tighter melee collision: normal enemies only damage when physically touching.
- Floating joystick: touch anywhere on the game screen to place the joystick under your finger.
- Smaller, more detailed Trash Panda with a more grounded illustrated look.
- More layered illustrated environments, skyline depth, props, lighting and combat readability.
- Improved weapon HUD and projectile visuals.
